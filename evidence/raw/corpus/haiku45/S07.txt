The paper clip was invented in 1899 by Johann Vaaler, a Norwegian inventor who patented his simple wire design. His original clip featured overlapping loops that held papers together without damaging them. The design became popular across Europe and eventually America. The familiar form with rounded wire bends was perfected and mass-produced in the early 1900s. Simple and cheap to manufacture, paper clips revolutionized office organization. Today they remain unchanged from Vaaler's original concept, making them one of history's most enduring inventions.
