**1. Audit Your Current System**
Document all active issues, projects, and custom fields in your existing tracker. Create an inventory of metadata, workflows, and integration points you depend on.

**2. Select and Test the New Platform**
Evaluate potential replacements based on your team's workflow requirements and budget constraints. Request trial access and configure a test instance with sample data.

**3. Map Data Between Systems**
Create a comprehensive mapping document showing which fields in the old system correspond to fields in the new one. Identify any custom fields or data that won't transfer directly and plan manual workarounds.

**4. Export Historical Data**
Extract complete records from your current system in a standard format like CSV or JSON. Verify that the export includes all issues, comments, attachments, and metadata needed for historical reference.

**5. Prepare Data for Import**
Clean and transform your exported data to match the target system's schema and requirements. Handle encoding issues, field type conversions, and any missing or malformed records.

**6. Communicate the Timeline**
Announce the migration date to your entire team and explain why the change is happening. Provide documentation about the new system, access procedures, and any behavioral differences.

**7. Import Data to New System**
Load the transformed data into the new tracker and verify that records were imported correctly. Check for missing data, duplicate entries, or formatting problems that require remediation.

**8. Configure Workflows and Automation**
Rebuild your issue status workflows, transitions, and automated actions in the new system. Test that notifications, escalations, and other integrations function as expected.

**9. Migrate Integrations**
Update third-party tools, CI/CD pipelines, and custom scripts that interact with your issue tracker. Verify that GitHub, Slack, email integrations and other connections work properly.

**10. Train Your Team**
Conduct hands-on training sessions covering daily workflows in the new system. Create quick-reference guides for common tasks like creating issues, assigning work, and searching.

**11. Run Parallel Testing**
Keep both systems operational during an initial testing period while your team acclimates. Monitor for issues or missing functionality that emerged during the dry run.

**12. Decommission Old System**
Archive data from the legacy tracker for compliance and reference purposes. Turn off access to the old system once everyone confirms the migration succeeded.
