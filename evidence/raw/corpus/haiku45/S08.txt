Traveling through Provence reveals the essence of southern France. The lavender fields near Valence create a purple haze across the landscape. In Aix-en-Provence, elegant eighteenth-century architecture surrounds intimate plazas where locals sip café at outdoor tables. The château ruins dotting the region tell stories of medieval nobility. Avignon's papal palace stands as a testament to ecclesiastical power. The cuisine blends Provençal herbs, fresh vegetables, and regional wines. Every village offers trattorias serving authentic coq au vin and ratatouille beneath mimosa trees.
