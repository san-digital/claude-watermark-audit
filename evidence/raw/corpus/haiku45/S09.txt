Marcus opened the café door and spotted Elena at their usual table. "You're late," she said, though her smile betrayed her. "I know, I know. Traffic was terrible." He sat down, stealing a croissant from her plate. "That's mine!" she protested, swatting his hand away playfully. "Was yours," he replied. Elena leaned back in her chair (a habit she'd developed years ago) and shook her head. "You haven't changed a bit since college." Marcus grinned. "Neither have you, and I wouldn't want either of us to."
