Marta's key stuck in the lock again, the way it always did in damp weather.

"You're back early," Ivo said, without looking up from the stove.

"The gallery shut at four. Something about a burst pipe on the floor above."

He turned the heat down and nodded slowly. "Then you'll have time to read the letter."

It was on the table, propped against the salt cellar (they had never got round to owning a proper letter rack), and she recognised the handwriting long before she was close enough to read her own name on the envelope.
