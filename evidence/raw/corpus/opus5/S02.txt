Two months. Two whole months that bike has been propped in the hallway with a flat back tyre, silently judging me every time I squeezed past it with the shopping. Today, in absolutely torrential rain, I finally did the thing. Tyre levers, patch kit, frozen fingers, water running down the back of my neck. Found the culprit as well — a sliver of glass barely bigger than a grain of sand. Rode it round the block afterwards purely to prove a point to nobody. Soaked, smug, sorted.
