We began in Nîmes, where the Roman arena still fills for summer concerts, then drove east towards Aix-en-Provence for a café and an hour of doing nothing in particular. The Rhône ran low and green beneath the bridges. At Saint-Rémy-de-Provence we ate under plane trees while old men played pétanque with tremendous seriousness, and afterwards we climbed to a ruined château above the Lubéron. Cézanne's mountain seemed to follow us for days. We finished with a cold rosé near the Camargue, watching flamingos wade through the salt marshes as the light went.
