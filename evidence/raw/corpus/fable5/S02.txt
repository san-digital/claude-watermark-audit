Finally fixed the puncture that's kept my bike in the shed for three weeks, and naturally I chose the rainiest Sunday of the year to do it. Wrestled the tyre off on the kitchen floor, found the tiniest shard of glass known to science, patched the tube, and only pinched it once putting everything back together. Took a victory lap around the block in the drizzle, soaked through and grinning like an idiot. There is no smugness quite like fixing something with your own hands. Tea has been earned. The bike lives.
