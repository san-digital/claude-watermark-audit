We drove through Provence in early June, when the lavender around the abbey of Sénanque was just turning purple. From Nîmes we took slow back roads to Aix-en-Provence, pausing at a shaded café for rosé and tapenade. We climbed to a ruined château above the Luberon, wandered the markets of L'Isle-sur-la-Sorgue, and ate daube provençale in a quiet square in Arles. Every village smelled of thyme and warm stone. By the time we reached Sète, sunburnt and content, we had already promised ourselves the same trip next year.
