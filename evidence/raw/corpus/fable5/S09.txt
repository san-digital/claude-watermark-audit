Marta's torch flickered as they reached the cellar door.

"You're sure about this?" she whispered.

"No," Tom admitted, "but we've come too far to turn back now."

She tried the handle (it turned far too easily for a door that had been locked for fifty years) and felt the cold air rise to meet them.

"Then after you, brave one," she said.

The darkness swallowed his answer whole.
