Mara set her mother's old teapot on the table. "It still works," she said, "even after all these years." Dev raised an eyebrow. "You're braver than me, trusting that thing near boiling water." "It hasn't exploded yet," Mara laughed, filling two cups (though the chip on the rim always worried her a little). Dev took his, blowing across the surface. "To your mother's terrible taste in kitchenware," he said, and they both laughed harder than the joke deserved, steam curling between them like something almost sacred.
