Our week in Provence began in Aix-en-Provence, wandering shaded boulevards lined with plane trees before stopping at a café for a café au lait and a warm croissant. We drove south to Nîmes to see the Roman arena, then east toward the hilltop village of Gordes, its stone houses glowing amber at dusk. An evening at a small château near Ménerbes brought a dinner of ratatouille and rosé, and a walk through lavender fields that still carried their late-summer scent. Arles, with its Van Gogh connections, rounded off a trip steeped in history, light, and unmistakably Provençal flavour.
